copy
AgentCode is agent-only in FORM, not secret from the owner. It is NOT encrypted, NOT obfuscated with a secret key. Full spec: AGENT-CODE.md. Full instructions: INSTRUCTIONS-FOR-AGENTS.md. Both are in this site and in the repo.
codebook-short
Fields (code=value, separated by ;, one line, header ac:1)
a=agent | ro=role | t=task | s=status | x=ctx | i=in | o=out | v=verdict | f=conflict | n=next | d=done | b=blocked | y=verify | nt=note
Status (s)
td=todo | dg=doing | vf=verify | dn=done | bl=blocked
Proof (inside v=model:claim:proof-code)
cd=code | lv=live | ts=test | nn=none (unproven — say so, never dress it up; nn cannot resolve a conflict)
End-state rule
Last field must be exactly one of n (next, status td/dg/vf), d (done, status dn), b (blocked, status bl) — except compact end-state: status dn/bl with no terminal field, as in a Cloud single-line pack.
Reason codes (for b)
not-ac | missing-agent | missing-task | missing-status | status-not-legal | terminal-missing | terminal-status-mismatch | secret-pattern | proof-none-cannot-resolve | field-not-code-equals | verdict-malformed | blocked-malformed — reported as blocked: reason=CODE | need=VALUE, exactly as AgentLang.
Values
Identifiers and kebab-case tokens only, no sentences, no spaces around ; or =. A sentence belongs in an al:1 note or a linked artifact, referenced by id here. Unknown codes are ignored with a warning, they do not fail the whole record.
converter
No live calls, no keys. Input is ac:1, al:1, or JSON depending on the button. Secrets are blocked, never echoed. If a record does not decode with the codebook, do not trust it.
